Crisis team

  • Chairman / CEO: final authority
  • Crisis Lead (Head of Communications): owns the narrative
  • Chief and Technical Spokespersons: one voice
  • Legal, Quality, Animal Health, Finance, HR, IT heads: facts and decisions
  • Media, Digital, Regional and Government liaisons: execution
  • Documentation Officer: timestamped crisis log

Media golden rules

  • Acknowledge fast; silence breeds rumour
  • One authorised voice
  • Facts only; never speculate or blame
  • Empathy first, then facts, then action
  • Never “no comment”; give a time for the answer
  • Same facts in every outlet and language
