Critical evaluation

Assessment of 2024-25 performance, based on the annual report

7.5
out of 10

Strong on innovation and policy delivery; weaker on measurable outcomes and reach

What worked

  • Indigenous tech (GauSort, GAUCHIP, Shashthi) cuts import dependence
  • Procurement kept growing despite large stocks
  • First carbon-credit payments to dairy farmers
  • Mother Dairy F&V +16% turnover

Where it falls short

  • Co-op procurement +2% vs ~6% sector growth: share is slipping
  • Sexed semen: 3.65 of 20.06 lakh doses used (~18%)
  • White Revolution 2.0 progress against 75,000 target not reported
  • Carbon credits at pilot scale (1,000+ farmers)

The rating and gap analysis are an analytical judgement, not statements from NDDB.
