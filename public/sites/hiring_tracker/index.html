Virtual Recruiter hiring flow

Every step from a Round 1 shortlist to the new hire's first day: who does what, what the system does on its own, and where each file is saved. Pooja and Bhawna run every step from the Hiring Tracker and send emails from their own Gmail through the Virtual Recruiter Claude Project.

How emails go out. Once a row is approved, the Hiring Tracker puts the finished email in its Outbox tab. Pooja or Bhawna then send it from their own Gmail by asking the Virtual Recruiter Claude Project to "send my ready emails". Until Shreya switches on live sending, Claude saves each one as a draft in the sender's Gmail.

Round 1: Technical

Steps 1 to 4
  1. 01

    Add the shortlisted candidate

    Pooja or BhawnaAutomatic
    • Add a row in the Hiring Tracker once the candidate is shortlisted for Round 1.
    • Required, no defaults: name, email, phone, company (Agility AI or Lyfshilp Academy), category, role, sender (Pooja or Bhawna), process owner (Pooja or Bhawna), final approver (Shreya, Sharad or Aryan), Round 1 interviewer.
    • The system assigns a Candidate ID (AG-2026-001 or LA-2026-001) and creates the candidate's folder.

    If any required field is blank, nothing happens. The reason is written in the row's Notes and appears in the daily digest.

    Status after: Shortlisted R1
  2. 02

    Approve and send two slot options

    Pooja or BhawnaAutomatic
    • The process owner ticks Approval 1. The email appears in the Outbox tab, ready for the sender to send from their own Gmail through the Claude Project.
    • The system finds two free slots in the interviewer's calendar, within working hours and skipping holidays, and holds both so nobody else books them.
    • The email goes from the sender (Pooja or Bhawna): "You are shortlisted, please pick a slot", with a link to the slot picker.
    Status after: R1 slots sentApproval: Process owner
  3. 03

    Candidate picks a slot

    CandidateAutomatic
    • The candidate opens the link and chooses Slot 1, Slot 2, or "Neither works". No Google account needed.
    • On a pick, the system books the Technical round with a Google Meet link, invites the candidate and interviewer, releases the other hold and sends a confirmation from the sender.
    • "Neither works" alerts the sender to offer new slots. No answer within the reply window sends the sender a reminder and releases both holds.
    Status after: R1 scheduledSaved to: Interviews
  4. 04

    Record the Round 1 result

    Pooja or BhawnaAutomatic
    • Pass: set the status to Shortlisted R2 and fill in the Round 2 interviewer.
    • Not selected: set Rejected and approve. The regret email goes from the sender.
    Status after: Shortlisted R2 or Rejected

    Approval: Final approver
  3. 10

    Automatic

    • The selection email goes from the sender with the offer PDF and the Joining Details Form link.
    • The offer expires after 3 working days. A reminder goes out the day before; on expiry the status changes and the sender is told.
    Status after: Offer sentSaved to: Contracts
  4. 11

    Candidate accepts and submits joining documents

    CandidatePooja or BhawnaAutomatic

    • Uploads: cancelled cheque, PAN card, full Aadhaar card (front and back), passport-size photo. Plus a consent checkbox.
    • The system saves the uploads to the candidate's folder, adds the typed details to the Joining Details Register, and emails the sender a list of anything missing, with ID numbers masked to the last 4 digits.
    Status after: Docs submittedSaved to: Official Documents

Every working day at 8 am

A digest to Shreya: approvals waiting, rows stopped for missing data, today's interviews, unanswered slot links, offers expiring, and joining forms received.

Rules that always apply

  • Nothing goes to a candidate without the right approval ticked.
  • The same email is never sent twice. Every action is checked against the Audit Log first.
  • PAN, Aadhaar and bank numbers show only the last 4 digits in emails, the Tracker, logs and file names.
  • The Joining Details Register holds full numbers. Only Shreya, Sharad, Aryan, Pooja and Bhawna can open it.
  • No emojis and no long dashes in any email or letter.

Statuses in order

Shortlisted R1, R1 slots sent, R1 scheduled, Shortlisted R2, R2 slots sent, R2 scheduled, Selected, Offer sent, Offer accepted, Docs submitted, Docs verified, Contract sent, Contract signed, Joined

Exits at any point: Rejected, Offer declined, Offer expired, Withdrawn, On hold.
