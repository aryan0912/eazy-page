Round 1: Technical

Steps 1 to 4
  1. 01

    Add the shortlisted candidate

    Pooja or BhawnaAutomatic
    • Add a row in the Candidates tab once the candidate is shortlisted for Round 1.
    • Required: name, email, phone, company, category, role, sender, process owner, final approver, Round 1 interviewer.
    • The system assigns the Candidate ID and creates the candidate's private folder.

    If any required field is blank, nothing happens. The reason is written in the row's Notes.

    Status after: Shortlisted R1
  2. 02

    Approve and send two slot options

    Pooja or BhawnaAutomaticClaude Project
    • The process owner ticks Approval 1. The system finds two free slots in the interviewer's calendar and prepares the slot-choice email in the Outbox.
    • The sender sends it from their own Gmail through the Claude Project.
    Status after: R1 slots sentArrives in Phase 2
  3. 03

    Candidate picks a slot

    CandidateAutomatic
    • The candidate chooses Slot 1, Slot 2 or "Neither works" from a link. No Google account needed.
    • The system books the Technical round with a Google Meet link and prepares the confirmation in the Outbox.
    • "Neither works" or no reply in time alerts the sender.
    Status after: R1 scheduledToday the time and Meet link are typed in by hand
  4. 04

    Record the Round 1 result

    Pooja or BhawnaAutomatic
    • Pass: set Shortlisted R2 and fill the Round 2 interviewer.
    • Not selected: set Rejected and approve. The regret email is prepared in the Outbox.
    Status after: Shortlisted R2 or Rejected

Round 2: Cultural Fit

Steps 5 to 7
  1. 05

    Approve and send two Round 2 slots

    Pooja or BhawnaAutomatic
    • Same as step 2, using the Round 2 interviewer's calendar and the Cultural Fit email.
    Status after: R2 slots sent
  2. 06

    Candidate picks a Round 2 slot

    CandidateAutomatic
    • Same as step 3: the Cultural Fit round is booked with a Meet link and the confirmation is prepared.
    Status after: R2 scheduled
  3. 07

    Final decision

    Pooja or Bhawna
    • Selected: move to the offer steps. Not selected: regret email, as in step 4.
    Status after: Selected or Rejected

Offer and joining documents

Steps 8 to 11
  1. 08

    Fill in the offer details

    Pooja or Bhawna
    • Stipend or CTC, pay period, start date, end date (interns and fixed-term), reporting manager, probation, work mode, signatory.
    • The process owner ticks Approval 1.
  2. 09

    Final approval

    Final approver
    • The final approver named on the row ticks Approval 2. The system checks who ticked it.

    No offer letter or contract is ever issued without this approval.

  3. 10

    Selection email, offer letter and joining form

    AutomaticClaude Project
    • The offer letter is made from the company's approved template and saved as a PDF.
    • The selection email with the offer and the Joining Details Form link waits in the Outbox for the sender.
    • The offer expires after 3 working days, with a reminder the day before.
    Status after: Offer sentArrives in Phase 3
  4. 11

    Candidate accepts and submits joining documents

    CandidatePooja or BhawnaAutomatic
    • The candidate signs in with their personal Google account and enters address, PAN, Aadhaar and bank details, and uploads a cancelled cheque, PAN card, Aadhaar card and passport photo.
    • Files go to the candidate's folder; details go to the restricted Joining Details Register.
    Status after: Docs submitted

Contract and joining

Steps 12 to 14
  1. 12

    Verify documents

    Pooja or Bhawna
    • Check the uploads against the typed details, then tick Docs verified.
    Status after: Docs verified
  2. 13

    Approve and send the contract

    Final approverAutomatic
    • Internship Agreement, Appointment Letter, Fixed-Term Appointment Letter or Consultancy Agreement, by category.
    • Governing law India; jurisdiction the courts at Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh.
    Status after: Contract sent
  3. 14

    Signed contract back, then Day 1

    CandidatePooja or BhawnaAutomatic
    • Pooja or Bhawna saves the signed copy and ticks Contract signed.
    • The Day 1 welcome email is prepared and the person is added to the Employee Register.
    Status after: Contract signed, then Joined
