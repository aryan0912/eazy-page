SWOT – Bank of Baroda

Strengths/Weaknesses are drawn from the reported figures above. Opportunities/Threats are analytical interpretations, not documented facts.

Strengths Fact

  • Record standalone net profit ₹19,581 cr, up 10.1% YoY
  • ROA 1.16%; ROE 16.96%
  • GNPA improved to 2.26%
  • Retail loans up 19.4% YoY; global deposits up 10.3%

Weaknesses Fact

  • Global NIM 3.02%, down from 3.18% in FY24
  • GNPA 2.26% vs SBI 1.82%; ROE below SBI’s 19.87%
  • Scale is smaller than SBI (total business about 28% of SBI’s, calculated)

Opportunities Interpretation

  • Digital self-service quality, if measured, can lift satisfaction at the highest-volume touchpoints
  • Retail growth gives a larger customer base to listen to systematically

Threats Interpretation

  • Margin pressure across the sector may limit investment capacity
  • Unaddressed digital-failure complaints could escalate into public reputation issues

Methodology & sources

  • Reported (official results, FY 2024–25): BoB – net profit ₹19,581 cr, operating profit ₹32,435 cr, global advances ₹12,30,461 cr, global deposits ₹14,72,035 cr, global NIM 3.02%, ROA 1.16%, ROE 16.96%, GNPA 2.26%, CRAR 17.19% (Mar 2025). SBI – net profit ₹70,901 cr, operating profit ₹1,10,579 cr, gross advances ₹42,20,703 cr, deposits ₹53,82,190 cr, whole-bank NIM 3.09%, ROA 1.10%, ROE 19.87%, GNPA 1.82%, NNPA 0.47% (Mar 2025).
  • Calculated: Total business = advances + deposits (BoB ₹27.02 lakh cr; SBI ₹96.03 lakh cr, using gross advances). BoB/SBI ratio = 27.02 ÷ 96.03. Net sentiment = positive % − negative %, weighted by monthly volume.
  • Not available: BoB NNPA, SBI CRAR, NII for both banks, customer base, digital-transaction volumes, financial inclusion and ESG metrics were not verified for this build.
  • Caveat: Figures were taken from published results coverage (Bajaj Broking, Angel One, Finology, Hello Banker, Business Standard, Morung Express) and should be reconciled against the official Annual Reports (bankofbaroda.bank.in; sbi.co.in) before the Chairman’s presentation. BoB NIM is global; SBI is whole-bank; definitions are close but not identical.
  • Sentiment: all values are illustrative and generated for demonstration. No survey, rating or customer feedback has been collected.
