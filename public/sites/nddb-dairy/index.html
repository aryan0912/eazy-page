Management insights
Illustrative interpretation of the current demo dataset
  1. 01
    Track net farmer returns, not procurement price alone.

    A procurement-price increase may not offset feed and fodder inflation. Add a district-level indicative milk margin measure.

  2. 02
    Validate payment complaints against transaction data.

    Link DCS-wise payment schedules and actual credit dates to distinguish isolated complaints from systemic delays.

  3. 03
    Build trust through human-reviewed vernacular insights.

    Show source type, language confidence, sample size and reviewer status alongside each sentiment signal.

Suggested implementation sequence
PHASE 1
Data foundation

Define DCS/union hierarchy, data agreements and trusted economic sources.

PHASE 2
Pilot ingestion

Start with consented IVR/voice, meeting notes and verified records.

PHASE 3
Analytics & validation

Local-language models, human review and economic correlation testing.

PHASE 4
Scale & governance

Role-based dashboards, alert workflows, audits and model monitoring.

Data sources & governance
Configuration concept for a future production deployment
Potential source connectors
  • Consent-based WhatsApp / IVR voice submissions
  • DCS meeting minutes and grievance registers
  • AMCS procurement, quality and payment records (subject to approvals)
  • Verified cattle feed and fodder price surveys
  • Regional mandi, agriculture and local news feeds
Governance controls
  • Role-based access: NDDB, federation, union and authorised users
  • Consent, purpose limitation and retention controls
  • Mask personal identifiers and restrict raw audio access
  • Show source provenance, timestamp and confidence score
  • Human validation before escalation or policy action
Important prototype notice: This is a front-end demonstration. It does not scrape websites, receive WhatsApp/IVR messages, run AI sentiment analysis, connect to AMCS, or predict cattle sales/defaults. The displayed figures, regions, statements and alerts are synthetic placeholders and must not be treated as actual NDDB or cooperative data.
